This tool follows HTTP redirects (3xx status codes) and shows the complete redirect chain from the initial URL to the final destination URL.

  • Supports HTTP status codes 301, 302, 303, 307, 308
  • Maximum 20 redirects to prevent infinite loops
  • Shows response time for each step
  • No content download - headers only
